3. Strict Mandi Calculation & Access Rules

  • Admin-Only Standard Rates: Operators cannot edit rates on intake. Rates are locked to daily rates.MARGA & rates.MARGEE.
  • Net Weight Formula: Net Weight (kg) = Gross Weight - (Crates Count × Tare per crate).
  • Valuation Derivation: Batch Amount (₹) = Net Weight (kg) × Standard Rate (₹/kg).
  • Customer Privacy Rule: Customer creation modals strictly omit the physical address field.
